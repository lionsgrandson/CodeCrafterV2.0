# CodeCrafter SEO Growth Backlog

Technical SEO is only one part of organic growth. These items require real content, client permission, or external websites and therefore should be handled as an ongoing operating backlog rather than automated with fabricated material.

## Priority 1: authority and legitimate links

- Add a natural CodeCrafter developer credit/link on completed client sites where the client approves it and where the link is useful rather than forced.
- Ask design, branding, SEO, and automation partners for reciprocal project/case-study attribution only where there is a real collaboration.
- Keep LinkedIn, Google Business, social profiles, and business directories consistent with the CodeCrafter name, domain, services, phone, and public business details.
- Pursue relevant Israeli SMB/development directories and professional profiles rather than bulk low-quality directory submissions.
- Turn legitimate project launches or technical write-ups into linkable resources.

## Priority 2: case studies

Current case-study architecture should be expanded only when facts can be verified. Each new case study should contain:

1. Client/project context.
2. The actual problem or constraint.
3. What CodeCrafter built or changed.
4. Technical or UX decisions that are genuinely known.
5. A visible/verifiable outcome.
6. Screenshots or project branding when permission/assets exist.
7. Links to relevant CodeCrafter service pages.
8. No invented conversion, revenue, ranking, traffic, or efficiency numbers.

The September 2026 hardening adds dedicated Rainbow ASD and Shimon Cohen Photography case studies in Hebrew and English in addition to the existing case studies.

## Priority 3: useful informational content

Publish useful material slowly enough to keep quality high. A strong target is one substantial article every two to four weeks rather than bulk AI publishing.

Suggested Hebrew topics based on commercial intent around CodeCrafter services:

- כמה עולה בניית אתר לעסק בישראל ב-2026 ומה באמת משנה את המחיר?
- CRM מותאם אישית מול Monday, HubSpot או מערכת מדף: מתי כל אפשרות מתאימה?
- מתי עסק צריך מערכת מותאמת ולא עוד Excel או Google Sheets?
- אוטומציה לעסק: אילו תהליכים כדאי לאוטומט ואילו לא?
- כמה עולה לפתח מערכת עסקית מותאמת ומה צריך להכין לפני אפיון?
- React, WordPress או Wix לעסק: איך בוחרים לפי הצורך ולא לפי טרנד?
- מה צריך לבדוק לפני שמחברים WhatsApp, CRM, סליקה או מערכת דיוור?
- מה בודקים לפני העלאת אתר או מערכת לפרודקשן?

English equivalents should be written for genuine English-language search intent, not mechanically translated only to create more URLs.

## Priority 4: Search Console-driven iteration

Monthly:

- Export or review query/page data for the last 28 and 90 days.
- Find pages with impressions but weak CTR and verify whether title/description/search intent are actually mismatched before rewriting metadata.
- Find queries ranking roughly positions 8-25 and strengthen the page that already owns that intent instead of creating a competing duplicate page.
- Review newly indexed and excluded URLs.
- Review mobile Core Web Vitals groups.
- Check Manual actions and Security issues.

Quarterly:

- Compare branded vs non-branded organic growth.
- Review which service pages generate leads, not just impressions.
- Refresh pricing/year references where needed.
- Confirm all public business facts and `sameAs` profiles remain current.
- Audit old redirects and external project domains.

## Avoid

- Do not buy bulk backlinks.
- Do not publish dozens of near-duplicate city pages.
- Do not make every article target the same phrase as a service page.
- Do not change homepage positioning every few weeks based on one search result rewrite.
- Do not create fake client metrics or fake office locations.
- Do not use Search Console Removals as a substitute for correct HTTP status/noindex behavior.
